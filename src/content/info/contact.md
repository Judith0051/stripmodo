Questions, corrections, or a scam you think we should document? The editorial
team reads everything and answers what we can.

## Email

Write to **editorial@stripmodo.pages.dev**

Good reasons to write:

- **Corrections** — a stale price, a changed platform rule, a factual error.
  Please include the page URL and, where possible, a source for the correct
  information. Material corrections are handled first.
- **Questions** that our guides do not answer — the best ones get added to the
  [FAQ](/stripchat-token-faq/) after verification.
- **Scam reports** — new fake-generator, phishing or mod-APK funnels you have
  encountered. URLs and screenshots help us document patterns (we report, we
  do not engage with scammers ourselves).

## What we will never do

- Ask for your StripChat password, verification or 2FA codes, recovery
  phrases, payment details or identity documents. Anyone claiming to be us who
  asks for these is an impostor.
- Sell tokens, run draws or “verify your account” on behalf of anyone.
- Provide individual financial or legal advice. For account- or
  payment-specific problems, contact the platform’s official support and your
  payment provider — our guides show the reporting paths.

## Response times

We are a small team. Corrections are typically reviewed within a few days;
other mail within about a week. If your message involves an active account
compromise, **act first, write later** — follow the recovery steps in the
[account safety guide](/stripchat-account-safety/) before doing anything else.
