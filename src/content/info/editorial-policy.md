Stripmodo publishes independent, safety-first guides about StripChat tokens.
This page explains how our content is produced, checked and maintained, so you
can judge how much trust to place in it.

## Independence

Stripmodo is **not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by StripChat** or
any broadcaster. We do not sell tokens, we do not operate token draws, and we
do not accept payment to change our conclusions. Third-party websites mentioned
on this site (for example the draw sites named in our disclosures) are listed
as **examples for users to verify independently — not as endorsements**, and
they are never presented as official properties.

## How we research

Every guide starts from primary sources: the platform’s own public pages, the
visible mechanics of common scam funnels, and consumer-protection material
from public authorities such as the FTC and CISA. When we describe scam
patterns — fake generators, phishing pages, trojanized “mod APKs” — we describe
**how to recognize and avoid them, never how to replicate them**. We do not
publish instructions for hacking, bypassing payments or abusing any platform.

## Standards for claims

- **No guaranteed outcomes.** We never state or imply that an offer guarantees
  tokens, prizes or rankings. Promotions are described as chance-based and
  time-limited, because they are.
- **Dated price information.** Figures on our price guide are labelled
  approximate and carry an explicit “prices checked on” date.
- **Honest uncertainty.** Where we cannot verify a claim — for example the
  current operation of a third-party draw site — we say so on the page rather
  than guessing.
- **No fabricated proof.** We do not use invented testimonials, reviews,
  screenshots of balances, or fake user stories.

## External links

We link to two kinds of external sites: **authoritative sources** (consumer
protection agencies, security authorities) that support our safety guidance,
and **third-party token-offer sites** that appear only inside clearly labelled
disclosure contexts with instructions to verify their legitimacy, terms,
privacy policy and availability. We review external links on a regular cycle,
but websites change hands and content changes — inclusion is never a guarantee.

## Corrections

If you find an error — a stale price, a broken claim, a mischaracterized
policy — tell us via the [contact page](/contact/). Material corrections are
made promptly and the page’s “Last updated” date is changed when a correction
affects meaning.

## Authorship

Content is written and reviewed by the Stripmodo Editorial Team. We use
editorial tools to assist research and drafting; every published guide is
reviewed by an editor against this policy before publication, and re-reviewed
at least quarterly or when platform changes require it.
