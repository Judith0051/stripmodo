Stripmodo is a static informational website. We collect as little data as
possible, and this policy explains the few cases where information is
processed at all.

## What we collect directly

**Almost nothing.** This site has no accounts, no login, no comment system, no
contact form that stores data server-side, no analytics that identifies you,
and no advertising trackers of our own.

- **Server logs.** Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) processes standard HTTP
  request information (IP address, user agent, requested URL) to serve the
  site and for security. We do not combine these logs with other data.
- **Email.** If you write to us using the address on the
  [contact page](/contact/), we receive your email address and message
  contents and use them only to reply and improve the site. We do not add you
  to mailing lists.

## Cookies

This site sets no first-party tracking cookies. If any cookie notice is
legally required for your region, it is because an embedded third party (see
below) may set cookies when you interact with their content.

## Third-party embeds and links

- **YouTube embeds.** Each guide embeds a YouTube video. YouTube may set
  cookies and collect viewing data according to Google’s policies when the
  embed loads or plays. The video is loaded lazily and only on the pages that
  display it.
- **Third-party offer websites.** Links marked as third-party (for example
  the draw sites named in our disclosures) lead to websites we do not operate.
  Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies — read them
  before providing any information, and never share passwords, 2FA codes,
  payment details or recovery information with them.
- **Authority links.** Links to FTC, CISA, FBI IC3 and Google open those
  organizations’ sites, which have their own policies.

## Your choices and rights

- Use browser settings to block or clear cookies and to enable “Do Not Track”
  (this site makes no use of cross-site tracking to begin with).
- Depending on your jurisdiction (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA and similar laws), you may
  have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Since we hold almost
  none, most requests resolve to a simple confirmation — contact us and we
  will answer honestly.

## Changes

We update this policy when the site’s data practices change; the “Last
updated” date at the top always reflects the current version. Questions:
use the [contact page](/contact/).
