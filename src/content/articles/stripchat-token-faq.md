## How to use this FAQ

Answers here are deliberately short and direct. Two things to keep in mind:

- **This FAQ is informational, not financial or legal advice.** Token rules,
  promotions and prices belong to the platform and to third-party operators,
  and they can change without notice.
- **Verify inside your official account.** Any detail that affects your money
  — prices, bonus conditions, refund rules — should be confirmed on the
  platform’s own pages before you act.
- **Answers stay honest when the honest answer is uncomfortable.** Where the
  truth is “it depends”, “nobody can promise that” or “we cannot verify it”,
  that is what we write. Vague certainty is the native language of scam pages;
  it has no place here.

If you are completely new to the platform, read the
[StripChat beginner guide](/stripchat-beginners-guide/) first; it will make the
answers below much more concrete. If your question is urgent — a compromised
account or a stolen payment — skip ahead to the recovery answers and then to

## Free tokens and promotions — the key facts

Before the questions, the five facts that answer most of them:

1. **Real promotions exist but stay small.** Hourly draws of a few dozen
   tokens, creator giveaways, occasional platform campaigns. Nobody funds big
   giveaways out of generosity — real offers are marketing, and marketing has
   budgets.
2. **Winning is chance-based.** A legitimate offer never guarantees you will
   receive tokens. “Guaranteed 5,000 free tokens now” is a scam signature, not
   a promotion.
3. **Entry is cheap or free.** A username, maybe an email. Anything beyond
   that — passwords, codes, cards, installs — converts the “offer” into the
   product being sold: you.
4. **Third-party sites are third parties.** Including the draw sites in our
   [disclosure box](#disclosure). Not official, not verified by us, and your
   responsibility to check.
5. **Official channels beat DMs.** If a promotion is real, you will find traces
   of it where it should live — inside your account or on the organizer’s
   verified profile.
6. **Featured video:** the short video at the top of this page summarizes the
   same principles visually — worth sixty seconds before you dive into the
   written answers.

question entries below link back to the deeper guides whenever a short answer
is not enough.

## Safety, scams and your account — the key facts

- **No legitimate offer needs your secrets.** Password, verification codes,
  2FA codes, recovery phrases, payment details, identity documents: each of
  these in a “token” context is a red flag with zero exceptions.
- **“Stripchat hack” and “mod APK” content is a warning topic, not a method.**
  Our [scam warning guide](/stripchat-token-scams/) explains how those schemes
  monetize and what malware-labeled mods actually do.
- **Recovery beats regret, but speed matters.** The
  [account safety guide](/stripchat-account-safety/) walks through password
  resets, session revocation, bank calls and reporting paths step by step.
- **Your device is part of your account.** Sideloaded apps and permissive
  browser extensions can hand strangers everything you type — everywhere, not
  just on one platform.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do StripChat free token methods really work?

Some promotional methods genuinely distribute small amounts of tokens — for
example hourly draws, creator giveaways and official in-account promotions.
There is no method that generates unlimited tokens: balances live on
StripChat’s servers, and only the platform can change them. Treat any tool
claiming otherwise as a scam.

### Is there a real Stripchat hack or mod APK for free tokens?

No. Modified APKs and so-called hacks cannot change server-side balances.
Files advertised this way commonly contain malware, steal login credentials
or intercept verification codes. Never install software or profiles in
exchange for promised tokens — see the [scam guide](/stripchat-token-scams/)
for how these schemes actually work.

### What information may a legitimate token offer ask for?

At most, a username and an email address for draw entry. A legitimate offer
never asks for your password, 2FA or verification codes, payment card
details, identity documents or recovery information. If it does, leave
immediately.

### Are third-party hourly draw sites safe to use?

They vary and we cannot verify them for you. Some run real small draws;
others mainly harvest data or push aggressive ads. Check their terms, privacy
policy and reputation, enter with a dedicated email, and never provide
passwords, codes, cards or software installs. Third-party sites — including
the examples in our [disclosure](#disclosure) — are not official StripChat
properties.

### How much do StripChat tokens cost?

It depends on package size, region, currency, payment method and current
promotions. Larger packages usually cost less per token. Any price outside
your own account page is an approximation, so check the purchase screen in
your account for current figures — and see our
[token prices guide](/stripchat-token-prices/) for dated, approximate ranges.

### Do StripChat tokens expire?

Purchased token balances do not normally expire quickly, but promotional or
bonus tokens can carry conditions, and platform rules can change. Check the
terms shown in your account, especially for bonus credits received through
promotions.

### Can I get a refund for tokens?

Rules depend on the platform’s terms, the payment method and your region.
Purchased tokens are often non-refundable once credited; disputes are usually
handled through official support or your payment provider. Keep receipts and
transaction IDs.

### How do I know a token giveaway message is real?

Verify it independently: check the announcement on the creator’s real profile
or inside your official account, and never confirm a win by clicking a link
or providing credentials. Real giveaways never DM you asking for passwords,
codes or card details.

### What should I do if I gave my password or code to a token site?

Change your password immediately from a trusted device, log out all sessions,
enable available security options, and watch your balance and statements. If
you shared card details, contact your bank. Report the scheme to the FBI IC3
([ic3.gov](https://www.ic3.gov/)) in the US or your local consumer authority.
The [safety guide](/stripchat-account-safety/) has the full checklist.

### Is this website affiliated with StripChat?

No. Stripmodo is an independent informational project. We are not affiliated
with, endorsed by or sponsored by StripChat, and the third-party offer sites
mentioned on this site are not official StripChat properties either. Always
verify offers through official channels — see our
[editorial policy](/editorial-policy/) and [disclaimer](/disclaimer/).

### What does “Stripchat free TK” mean in posts and comments?

“TK” is shorthand for tokens; “free TK” posts advertise draws, giveaways or
(very often) scam funnels. Judge each by the same checks: small chance-based
prizes with published rules can be legitimate; guaranteed large amounts,
downloads, surveys or requests for credentials are scams.

### Can I use free-token offers from any country?

Not always. Promotions are frequently region-limited, and third-party draw
sites may accept entries only from certain countries. Check the offer’s
eligibility rules; pretending to be elsewhere (VPN tricks, false profiles)
can violate terms and void winnings.

### How long do draw winnings take to arrive?

Legitimate operators publish a crediting window, commonly 24–72 hours,
sometimes longer. A delay inside the stated window is normal; silence followed
by requests for fees, codes or logins is a scam pattern, not a delay — consult
the [scam guide](/stripchat-token-scams/) if that happens.

### Is it legal to use third-party token draw sites?

We cannot give legal advice and rules differ by country. In many places,
entering a promotional draw is lawful, but the site’s own compliance is its
operators’ responsibility, and use must also respect the platform’s terms.
Verify legality and legitimacy yourself before participating.

### Why do token prices differ between users?

Pricing is localized: region, currency, taxes, payment method and targeted
promotions all change the number on your screen. Screenshots of other people’s
prices are weak evidence; your account’s purchase page is the only reliable
figure — context is in the [prices guide](/stripchat-token-prices/).

### Do I have to pay to enter legitimate token draws?

No. A genuine free-token draw is free to enter — at most it asks for a
username and an email. Any entry fee, “verification deposit” or card
requirement converts the offer into a purchase or a scam, and should be
refused.

### Can I gift tokens to another user?

Gifting tokens is generally done by tipping the broadcaster directly or
through platform-provided mechanics. Never send tokens or payments outside
the platform to “transfer” a balance — those arrangements are a common scam
and may violate platform terms.

### What happens if a draw site I entered shuts down?

Third-party sites appear and disappear constantly; it is inherent to the
category. If one closes, any pending entries are gone — which is why you
should never pay to enter, never reuse your main email, and never let a draw
site hold anything you cannot afford to lose.

### Can I watch StripChat without ever buying tokens?

Yes. Public rooms are free to watch, and a viewer who never spends is doing
nothing wrong. Tokens buy interaction and exclusivity — tips, requests,
private and spy shows — not basic admission. The
[beginner guide](/stripchat-beginners-guide/) explains what is free and what
is not.

### Should I use my main email for token offers and draws?

Better not to. Use a dedicated email address for draw entries and promotional
signups: it keeps phishing attempts isolated, protects your main inbox, and
makes it obvious which messages pretend to be winner notifications.

## Where to verify things yourself

Good answers expire; verification skills do not. These are the places that
tell you the current truth:

- **Your account pages** — for prices, balances, promotions you personally
  qualify for, security settings and session lists.
- **Official support channels** — for account-specific problems; never for
  “verification” of third-party offers, which support cannot audit either.
- **The offer’s own terms and privacy policy** — for entry rules, crediting
  windows and operator identity. Absent paperwork is an answer too.
- **Public protection resources** — the FTC and CISA guidance linked below,
  and [Google Safe Browsing](https://transparencyreport.google.com/safe-browsing/search)
  for a site’s current blocklist status.
- **Independent discussion** — long-running community threads about a site or
  promotion, read with an eye for astroturfing.
